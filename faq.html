Frequently asked questions

Delivery, cash on delivery, returns, payment, sizing, and how we stand behind the bottle.

Standard delivery is 3–5 working days for major cities and a little longer for Gilgit and Muzaffarabad. It is free when your order is Rs. 5,000 or more. Otherwise it is Rs. 250, with a small extra on some farther cities. Express is 1–2 days and adds Rs. 400. You’ll see an estimate on the product page once you pick a city, and again at checkout.

COD is available nationwide. Pay the rider after you have looked at the parcel. If you can, keep the amount ready. We don’t ask for an advance on COD orders. If a rider cannot reach you after two attempts, the order returns and we’ll WhatsApp you.

You have 7 days from delivery. The piece should be unworn, unwashed, and in its tags. Perfumes can only come back if the seal is intact. Stitched salwar kameez can be exchanged for another size if it hasn’t been worn; unstitched fabric is returnable if it is uncut. Start the return on WhatsApp with your order ID and a photo. Refunds for prepaid orders go back to the same channel. COD refunds are transferred to JazzCash, Easypaisa, or your bank.

Pay with cash on delivery, JazzCash, Easypaisa, or a Meezan Bank transfer. Card details on the checkout page are a demonstration only — this static shop does not charge a card and does not store the number. For JazzCash, Easypaisa, and bank transfer, send the total and upload the screenshot so we can match it to your order ID. Promo codes: NAWAB10 takes 10% off, WELCOME15 takes 15% off.

Every product page has a size guide. T-shirts and kurtas list chest, length, and shoulder in inches. Jeans are waist sizes 28–38 — measure a favourite pair laid flat and double it. Hats are free size. Salwar kameez runs S–XL when stitched. Unstitched sets include the meterage in the details so your tailor can cut to you. If you are between sizes in a kurta, take the larger one.

Nawab Sahab perfume is filled and packed for us in Lahore. It is not a rebottled designer fragrance. Clothing is cut for this label. If a batch number or stitch looks wrong, send a photo and we will replace it. We would rather take a piece back than argue.

Use the order ID from your confirmation (it looks like LX-2026-12345) and the mobile number you checked out with. On this demo the order lives in your browser, so track it on the same phone or computer. Status moves from Placed to Confirmed, Packed, Shipped, and Delivered based on how long ago you ordered.
