The house

About Nawab Sahab

We make clothes and fragrance for people who live in Pakistani cities and still want the thing to feel considered.

The story

Nawab Sahab started as a small edit between a lawn season and a bottle of oud that didn’t smell like anyone else’s. The brief was simple: pieces you can wear to a dholki, a Friday, or a late dinner in Karachi, without a logo doing the talking.

We stitch in Lahore and Karachi, print lawn with mills we can visit, and fill our perfumes ourselves. Prices are in rupees. Delivery is cash on delivery because that is how people here actually shop.
